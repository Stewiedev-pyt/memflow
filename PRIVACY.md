# Privacy Policy — Stewie Agent

**Last updated:** August 2026

## 1. Overview

The Stewie agent is designed to minimize data collection. We only process the data necessary to fulfill your requests.

## 2. Data We Collect

### During Usage
- **Query text**: The commands you send (e.g., "scan PEPE") are processed in real-time and are **not stored permanently**
- **Job results**: Returned to you and stored temporarily (1 hour max) for job status polling, then deleted

### Via Masumi Network
- **Wallet address**: Your Cardano wallet address used for payment transactions is handled entirely by the Masumi Network, not by us
- **Payment records**: Managed by Masumi's blockchain infrastructure

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
- Login credentials
- IP addresses
- Browser fingerprints
- Trading history or portfolio data
- Wallet private keys

## 4. Third-Party APIs

The agent queries the following third-party services to retrieve public blockchain data:
- DexScreener (token data, pair information)
- GeckoTerminal (OHLCV, pool trades)
- Helius (Solana RPC, token accounts)
- Etherscan (Ethereum transfers, contract creators)
- CoinGecko (market data, Fear & Greed Index)
- CryptoPanic (news aggregation)
- Honeypot.is / RugCheck (security audits)

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control their data collection practices.

## 5. Data Storage

- No user accounts or profiles are created
- No cookies are used
- Job data is held in memory for up to 1 hour, then purged
- No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties

## 6. Blockchain Transparency

All payments are processed on the Cardano blockchain via the Masumi Network. Transaction amounts and wallet addresses are publicly visible on-chain as part of normal blockchain operation. This is inherent to blockchain technology, not a data collection practice.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Stewie agent is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
